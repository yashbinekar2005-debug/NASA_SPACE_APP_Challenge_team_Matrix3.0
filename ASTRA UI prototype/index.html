    How the score and the sites are produced
    • Earth screening cells come from a target-independent global land grid. NASA POWER climate is retrieved first; cells are then enriched with SRTM relief, SoilGrids texture, Macrostrat geology and reverse-geocoded labels where available. A missing source is shown as unavailable and never replaced with a guessed value.
    • No analog site is predefined. Place names are labels from a reverse geocoder; every factor value comes from the dataset named in the evidence trail.
    • Clicking any visible point on the Moon or Mars globe probes that exact coordinate through the server: LRO LOLA relief, LRO Diviner hourly temperatures, Kaguya MI FeO, MOLA relief, USGS SIM3292 geology and the Mars Climate Database. The measured values enter the same seven-factor model as a published target.
    • Mars Climate Database values are a model climatology, not surface-station measurements, and the interface labels them that way. Seasonal spread is shown separately and is not a published model error.
    • If a product fails, is outside its coverage, or is not connected, that factor is returned as unavailable, excluded from the score and listed as a warning. Missing data is never replaced by a latitude guess or a synthetic value.
    • Each factor becomes a bounded distance between 0 and 1, using a documented cap. Temperature uses 200 °C, solar irradiance 12 kWh/m²/day, and the 0–100 indexes use their own scale.
    • Similarity = 100 × (1 − weighted mean distance). Your sliders set the weights.
    • Missing or non-comparable factors are excluded and the remaining weights are renormalised; coverage is reported separately.
    • Lava-tube access is only counted when the selected target has a mapped tube or pit, and it is a capability proxy, not a surveyed cave.
    • Confidence combines coverage, documentation quality and cross-body comparability. It is not the same thing as similarity.
    • Uncertainty is propagated from the target and site measurement errors. The ± value and the 95 % interval describe the score, not the quality of the site.
    • Gravity, vacuum, radiation, magnetic field, day length and atmosphere cannot be reproduced by choosing a different Earth site. The Compare screen lists them explicitly.

    Earth candidates are globally generated screening-grid cells from NASA POWER, ISRIC SoilGrids, SRTM, Macrostrat and a reverse geocoder. Rankings recalculate from the selected planetary coordinate and current weights. Published targets use documented measurements. Points you click are probed live from LRO LOLA, LRO Diviner, Kaguya MI, MOLA, USGS SIM3292 and the Mars Climate Database; MCD values are modelled climatology. Real 3×3 DEM samples replace the generated terrain preview for probed points.
