    Small bars show how well each factor matches, left to right: temperature, ice, soil, terrain, dryness, lava tube.

    How the score works
    • Each factor gets a similarity from 0 to 100.
    • Temperature: 100 × (1 − gap ÷ 200 °C).
    • Other factors: 100 − the gap between two 0 to 100 indexes.
    • Lava-tube access only counts when the target has a tube.
    • The score is the average of the factors, weighted by your sliders.
    • Earth's gravity, air and magnetic field are not modeled. No site can match them.
